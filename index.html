UNT's Strategic Retention Dashboard: 2027-28 FTIC Cohort

Predictive Analytics & Strategic Student Retention Platform

1. Dashboard Navigation & Analytics Instructions

  • Global Risk Cohort Filter: Select a risk quartile from the top dropdown control ("HIGH RISK STUDENTS", "MEDIUM RISK STUDENTS", etc.) to dynamically update all KPI metric cards, student rosters, risk drivers, and retention intervention strategies.
  • Interactive Roster Search & Pagination: Use the search bar above the student table to filter high-priority students by name, college, or major in real time. Use pagination controls to navigate through student rosters smoothly.
  • Top Risk Drivers & Strategic Retention Interventions: Located in the right-hand panel, this view loads the statistical attrition drivers and targeted strategic interventions corresponding strictly to the selected cohort.

2. Updating Process (Fresh Data Ingestion)

To refresh this interactive dashboard with updated student data and UNT administrative data or new risk model scoring outputs without needing an external web server or backend codebase:

  1. Click the "Upload CSV Data" button in the top right header navigation bar.
  2. Upload your updated CSV files corresponding to the main dataset (e.g., Dashboard_MockUp_Data_data_tab.csv) and strategic interventions dataset (e.g., Dashboard_MockUp_Data_StrategicHRInterventions_tab.csv).
  3. Click "Apply Updated Data" to render a fresh real-time analysis immediately.
